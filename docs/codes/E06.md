## What happened

The supervisor lost power and started up again. On boot it found two things true
at once: the state it had saved was `TRIPPED`, and the winding was still at or
above `RESET_THRESHOLD` — 70 °C. So it came up tripped instead of armed, left its
relay open, and logged `BOOT_HOT_HOLD`.

Nothing is broken and nothing new went wrong. The trip you already had is still
in force, because the motor is still hot.

## Before you touch anything

Two different rules apply here, and mixing them up is the hazard.

- **Waiting needs the disconnect on.** The supervisor is powered from downstream
  of it and cannot run the cooldown timer without power.
- **Cleaning the fan shroud afterwards needs the disconnect locked out.** By then
  the relay has re-closed, and a press of Start spins the fan.

## Do this now

1. Leave the machine disconnect **on**. The supervisor is powered from
   downstream of it and needs power to watch the motor cool and to run the
   cooldown timer.
2. Wait. The fast blink becomes a slow blink when the winding drops below 70 °C,
   then goes solid 120 seconds after that.
3. Press Start.
4. Before the next long session, clean the fan shroud on the back of the motor.
   The trip that got you here almost certainly came from a blocked shroud —
   [E01](E01.md) has the procedure, including the lockout it requires.

## Why it happens

Usually because someone flipped the disconnect off and back on to reset the saw.
That is a reasonable thing to try — it clears plenty of machines. It does not
clear this one, and that is deliberate.

Trips are written to the ESP32's flash as they happen, so they survive power
loss. On the next boot the supervisor reads that back and compares it against a
fresh temperature reading. If the last state was a trip and the winding has not
yet fallen below 70 °C, it holds the trip.

The reason is simple: cutting power for five seconds changes the state of the
electronics and nothing else. The winding that hit `TRIP_THRESHOLD` (110 °C) is
still carrying that heat. A 3 HP TEFC — Totally Enclosed Fan-Cooled — motor is a
lump of iron and copper that sheds heat slowly through its finned frame, and this
one has a service factor of 1.0, so there is no thermal margin to gamble with. If
a reboot cleared the trip, you could restart into a winding sitting a few degrees
below the trip point and be back over it inside one cut.

A power cycle is not the only way to get here. A supply blip in the shop, or the
supervisor's own watchdog resetting it during a trip, produces the same result.

Note that the LED shows the same fast blink for this as it does for a fresh
overtemperature trip — both are `TRIPPED` on thermal cause. The dashboard log is
what distinguishes them.

## When it clears

Automatically, on temperature, exactly the way [E01](E01.md) does:

1. Winding falls below `RESET_THRESHOLD` (70 °C) → slow blink.
2. It stays below 70 °C for `COOLDOWN_HOLD` (120 seconds) → solid, and the relay
   re-closes.
3. **You press Start.**

The saw does not restart on its own. The relay closing only makes starting
possible again. The starter is a 3-wire momentary-Start / maintained-Stop circuit
with a seal-in auxiliary contact — once the coil circuit is broken the contactor
stays dropped until a human presses the physical Start button. That is a property
of the wiring (`SR-4`), not of the firmware.

Cycling power again does not speed any of this up. With the disconnect off the
supervisor is unpowered and is watching nothing; the motor cools at the same rate
either way, and you lose the log entries and restart the 120-second hold from
zero on the next boot.

## If it keeps happening

If you get this without anyone having touched the disconnect, the supervisor is
losing power on its own while the saw runs — a loose tap connection or a supply
fault. Repeated unexplained reboots are a builder task, not an operator one. The
log records every boot.

If it keeps happening because the motor keeps reaching 110 °C, the code you
actually need is [E01](E01.md) — clean the fan shroud and check the blade. Three
trips inside ten minutes will put the supervisor into `MANUAL_LOCKOUT`, which is
[E04](E04.md) and needs a physical acknowledgement to clear.
